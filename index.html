Research

I am interested in building embodied agents that can ground multimodal perception in physical constraints, reason over possible actions, and generalize reliably beyond their training distributions. My broader interests include VLA/VLN systems, world models, robot learning, and robust decision making.

Robust VLA Planning under Visual Distribution Shifts

Ongoing research

I study how visual appearance shifts affect end-to-end driving planners. This work decomposes planning degradation into trajectory support failure and decision corruption, motivating a framework based on Support Recovery and Certified Decision Repair. The aim is to recover reliable planning behavior using physically grounded evidence.
